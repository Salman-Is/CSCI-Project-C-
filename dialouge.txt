[NAR_INTRO]
Hear the bell, wanderer.
Long ago, before the roads had names, a crown was forged in Linaura, and it would bend to no head that was weak. 
The first to wear it was crowned in the ashes of a war, and he rose as an Earl. 
A divine mortal with the strength of ten thousand. A reign that outlasted the kingdoms around him.
When an Earl falls, the Sondering Bell tolls, and the crown lies open to any who dare. 
Ten times it has rung. Ten times the land has burned. 
Crownseekers have marched from every border to prove themselves in the old way, 
by blade, by spell, and by blood. Between the wars, Linaura knew centuries of quiet.

The tenth Earl, Elenora, ruled longest of all. Her name outlived her crown, and her reign wore her thin, 
until little was left of her but a title and a tired voice. In time, she too was gone.
The bell rings once more.

With no Earl to keep the world shut, the Defiled Region has torn open at the heart of the land.
The things from the underworld crawl out into the dark. 
Warbands from lands beyond cross the borders, and every road is a battlefield. 
In the high mountains, Castle Norn stands silent, its gates sealed. 
They will open only for one who has proven worthy of what lies within.

You are a warrior. You have no crown, no prophecy, and no claim, only a sword, a reason, and a long road.
Take the Earlcrown, end the cycle, or let it turn again. The bell is tolling.
